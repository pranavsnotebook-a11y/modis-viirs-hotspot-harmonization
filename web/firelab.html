Method, sources and limits · read before using
    This is a teaching and exploration tool. The plume is a scenario from a single satellite snapshot and published formulas. It has not been validated against measurements, and it must not be used for health or evacuation decisions. Use official air-quality services for those.
    Welcome to the FIRELAB simulation space
    1. Pick a region and a fire. Choose a region on the left, then click any orange dot on the map or pick from the list. Every dot is a real NASA FIRMS detection.
    2. Read the weather. The modelled wind at the satellite pass sets where the smoke goes. The cyan arrow on the map shows the direction.
    3. Play with the scenario. Drag the hours, change vegetation, try the worst case or your own wind. Press ▶ Play to watch the plume grow hour by hour.
    4. Watch the results. The cards and charts on the right update with the map. Pin a run as A to compare it with the next one.
    5. Remember what it is. Orange SCENARIO values are model estimates, not measurements. The method is at the bottom of the page.
