Method, sources and limits · read before using
    This is a teaching and exploration tool. The plume is a scenario from a single satellite snapshot and published formulas. It has not been validated against measurements, and it must not be used for health or evacuation decisions. Use official air-quality services for those. The wind and smoke animation redraws this scenario with time sped up; it is not a forecast or an observation.
    Welcome to the FIRELAB simulation space
    Five steps, about a minute.
    1. Pick a fire. Choose a region, then click any orange dot on the map or pick one from the list. Every dot is a real NASA FIRMS detection.
    2. Watch the wind. The moving streaks show the modelled wind at the satellite pass, which decides where the smoke goes.
    3. Change the scenario. Drag the hours, change the vegetation, or try the worst case. Press ▶ Play to watch the plume grow hour by hour.
    4. Read the results. The cards and charts on the right update with the map. Pin a run as A to compare it with the next.
    5. Know what it is. Orange SCENARIO values are model estimates, not measurements. The method is at the bottom of the page.
