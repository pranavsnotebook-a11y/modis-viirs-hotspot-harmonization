      What this is

      OBSERVED Fires are detected by satellites (NASA FIRMS, MODIS and VIIRS). Only fires recorded in this region are in this page's data; fires across the border are not, so near a border the page can miss a fire on the other side.

      MODELLED Wind comes from Open-Meteo weather models, at 100 m, for the hour of the satellite pass and the two hours after it.

      SCENARIO The smoke path is a FIRELAB Gaussian plume scenario. The page uses the wind at this place for fires up to 25 km away. "Downwind" means the scenario adds at least 1 µg/m³ of fine particles at this place. It is not a measurement.

      It is not a health, visibility or evacuation forecast and not an operational alert service. Check with people on the ground.

      Your location stays in this browser; the rounded coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get the wind.

      The same fire seen on several passes is shown once, with its latest pass. Next look is estimated from recent pass times, not an orbit prediction. Times use the time zone of the selected region (Brazil has several; the page uses Brasília time).
